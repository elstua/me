## Start with the decision

Write about the choice, constraint, or observation that made the work interesting. A concrete opening gives readers a reason to continue.

## Show what changed

Use images, short clips, or code alongside the story when they make the decision easier to understand.

## End with what you learned

Close with the principle you would carry into the next project—not just a recap of what happened.
